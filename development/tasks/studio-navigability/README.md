# `NAV-01` — making Studio navigable

**Read [`EXECUTION_CONTEXT.md`](EXECUTION_CONTEXT.md) first.** It carries the
live evidence, the target shell, five slices in order, and three open decisions
that must not be assumed.

## Why this package exists

Studio is honest and not navigable. Fourteen destinations, six of which have no
controls of their own; one list of 180 statements rendered on three different
pages; counts that disagree on a single screen; UUIDs in the leftmost column of
the page that explains what the project is.

The owner's own words, typed into the product's Workspace after 95 revisions of
real work: **"How do I proceed. It looks very complicated?"**

## Relationship to `studio-productization`

`PHASE_REGISTER.md` there defined `ES-3` (usable conversation workspace), `ES-4`
(actionable empty and blocked states) and `ES-5` (scalable Requirements and
Reviews). Those phases named the right problems from the outside. This package
is the same work re-derived **from a live sweep of the running product**, with
counts, control tallies and screenshots rather than expectations.

Where the two disagree, this one is the newer evidence. `ES-5`'s dependency on
KAE-Memory canonicalisation (`EM-3`) still stands and is why `OD-NAV-2` exists.

## The rule this package must not break

The instinct to fix a wall of text is to hide some of it. Every capability note,
`Not yet` badge and *why it is not ready* paragraph in Studio was written
because a previous version claimed something it could not do.

**Group, rank, and paginate. Do not delete a truth.**
